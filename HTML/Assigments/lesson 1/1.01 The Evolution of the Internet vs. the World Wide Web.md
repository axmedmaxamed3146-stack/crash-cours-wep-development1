1. Internet Mapping Activity — Answer
Data travels from my computer to my friend's computer through several devices and networks.
My Computer
     ↓
Wi-Fi Router
     ↓
Internet Service Provider (ISP)
     ↓
Internet Routers
     ↓
International Network / Fiber-Optic Cables
     ↓
Friend's ISP
     ↓
Friend's Router
     ↓
Friend's Computer
Explanation:
When I send data to my friend's computer, the data first leaves my computer and goes to my Wi-Fi router. The router sends the data to my Internet Service Provider (ISP). The data then travels through different routers and network connections across the Internet. If my friend is in another country, the data may travel through international fiber-optic cables. Finally, the data reaches my friend's ISP, then their router, and arrives at their computer.
